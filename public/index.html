How the meter works

Each signal is scored −100 to +100 from its 4-week move against its own past year of moves. Signals: growth (copper), rates & credit (US 10Y inverted, high-yield credit), dollar (DXY inverted), risk appetite (VIX inverted, S&P 500), energy (Brent, TTF), China (China equities, yuan inverted, iron ore), commodity breadth. Above +10 risk-on, below −10 risk-off. Completed weeks are frozen, so the record can't be rewritten.
